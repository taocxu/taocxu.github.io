Publications

  1. "Towards Sustainable Prosperity? Policy Evaluation of Jiangsu Advanced Manufacturing Clusters,"
    with Yabei Hu*, honoured by Jiangsu Provincial Distinguished Award
    Technology in Society, 2024, 77, 102583  [PDF]

      2. The government of China, to hone manufacturing's competitive edge, has adopted a series of regional industrial policies to stimulate advanced manufacturing clusters as facilitators of agglomeration effects, utilisers of factor endowments, and implementers of innovation solutions, by playing the role of facilitating state. Focusing on advanced manufacturing cluster policies in East China's Jiangsu Province, the research employs a mixed data analysis methodology on a sample of 52 documents collected from the Jiangsu government and affiliated units. The policy orientations and priorities are examined under the triple analytical framework in terms of themes, instruments, and controls, applying textual mining and the PMC index model. The results reveal the alignment of policy themes with high-quality development strategy, the government preferences for the supply dimension in policy instruments, the emphasis on planning directive in policy controls, together with the relatively high internal consistency of the AMC policy toolkit by Jiangsu. Therefore, the research spotlights the importance of leveraging comparative strengths based upon the facilitating state institution, and of deploying internally coherent, scientific, and efficient initiatives that exploit more potential of advanced manufacturing clusters to foster sustainable prosperity from the perspective of new structural economics.

  2. "Mismatch and Dysfunction of the Ageing Economy: Digital Transformation in Silver-Hair Industry,"
    with Yukun Gu, Yansong Wang, and Qifei Chen,
    Journal of the Jiangsu Association for Commercial Economics, 2021, 38, 1–9  

      3. With the endeavours towards healthy ageing, China’s older demographic exhibits characteristics of a substantial scale, a swift rate of increase, and uneven distribution between urban and rural areas. Social and economic development has elevated the material conditions and living standards of city seniors, directing our focus on the heightened demand and inequality from this demographic. However, significant deficiencies in the myopic supply strategy of China’s pro-senior consumer market precipitate conspicuous mismatch and dysfunction. The mismatch and disparity between supply and demand feature increasingly manifest, with the market inadequately positioning the genuine needs of the older individuals across heterogeneous regions. Consequently, the emerging undersupply of the silver-hair industry necessitates technoscience-based improvement. 1) The economies of scale and the scalability of the Internet are expanding the pro-senior market, thus facilitating industrial development with digital transformation. 2) Then, leveraging big data mitigates the information asymmetry between the supply and demand perspectives. 3) Furthermore, e-commerce enables deeper, sustainable production and consumption, while 4) intelligent logistics handle the ‘last mile’ challenge in both urban-rural supply chains. Collectively, the digital synergy creates inclusive opportunities for optimisation, delivering our Internet-based pathway to rectify market mismatch and dysfunction.

  3. "Reshaping Silver-Hair Industry with New Retailing: A SWOT-Based Strategy,"
    Journal of the China Federation of Logistics, 2021, 28, 82–85  

      4. The silver-hair industry, a market segment that centres around consumption patterns and structures of older persons, is progressively accentuated within the ambit of an ageing society. Spurred on by socio-economic development and the assimilation of digital technology, China’s silver-hair industry is confronted with an exigency for digital transformation and consumer-centric marketing. New retailing is revolutionising business archetypes, augmenting the retail environment, and recalibrating the rapport between suppliers and consumers. Through the confluence of online and offline platforms with logistics, new retailing facilitates the resilience of entire industrial value chains. The data collection and analytics enable the optimisation of product configuration and consumer stewardship, thereby not only satiating the diverse demands of older individuals but transforming the outdated production and distribution processes within the silver-hair industry. This strategy amplifies the potential of the silver-hair economy by ushering in diversification, experiential amenities, cultural engagement, and social interactions. Via a SWOT-based analytical scaffold, this research articulates the vistas of new retailing, delineating the opportunities and impediments in praxis.

Work in Progress

  1. "It Takes Three to Ceilidh: Pension System and Multidimensional Poverty Mitigation in China,"
    with Yansong Wang*, and Cheng Yuan, arXiv 2411.02807, 2024  [PDF]

      2. This research, employing the Alkire-Foster approach to uncover multidimensional poverty between 2012 and 2020 in China, models and examines the sustainable effects and mechanisms of the three-pillar pension system in household poverty mitigation with the China Family Panel Studies data. The results evince that more participation in the pension system mitigates the probability of being trapped in multidimensional poverty. The findings reveal the significance of synchronising state social insurance, enterprise annuity, and individual commercial insurance. The mitigation effect of market-oriented pillars is achieved through more investment in and consumption for livelihood assets. Based upon the sustainable livelihoods framework, livelihood assets ameliorate household capabilities in human, natural, financial, and psychological capital against exogenous shocks and uncertainties. Our research contributes to the theory and praxis of the facilitating state in new structural economics illustrated by the multi-pillar pension system in sustainable micro-household poverty mitigation.
