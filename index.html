PUBLICATIONS

  1. ‘Towards Sustainable Prosperity? Policy Evaluation of Jiangsu Advanced Manufacturing Clusters’

    with Yabei Hu, Technology in Society, 2024, 77, 102583, honoured by Jiangsu Provincial Distinguished Award · [PDF]

    This research delivers a multifaceted evaluation of Jiangsu’s selective, meticulous industrial policy of development and innovation for sustainable prosperity.

    • Policy Text Evaluation: Examines 52 policy documents with a mixed analytical framework.
    • Strategic Focus: Interprets East China’s transition to a global manufacturing powerhouse, spotlighting industrial ecosystem-oriented development.
    • Socioeconomic Development: Details Jiangsu’s phased progress in cultivating industrial clusters and sustaining regional economic prosperity.

    The findings portray a blueprint for leveraging advanced manufacturing clustering to achieve regional sustainable growth. The research makes a constructive contribution by encouraging future work to investigate the evolving impact and critically analyse the sustainability outcomes of a revived but distinctive industrial policy toolkit amid global economic and socio-political shifts.
